Connect this boat

  1. Press Connect to yey. A link appears.
  2. Open the link (on your phone is fine), sign in to yey.boats with Google or Telegram, and confirm this boat.
  3. Come back here: this page switches to connected on its own.

Already added the boat on yey.boats or with /linkboat in the Telegram bot? Type the code it gave you instead.

A connection request is still waiting for you to confirm it on yey.boats. Open the link you were sent and confirm — this page finishes the pairing on its own, and it survives a reload.

What yey does

It reads the values you allow from this SignalK server and answers questions about your boat — where it is, what the wind and depth are doing, what happened on passage. What it sends is set by a subscription plan you manage on the web, and it can never exceed what you consented to.

Price

$0.99 per month plus VAT for the base plan. Bring your own Claude API key (BYOK) and there are no token purchases at all — you pay Anthropic directly. Pricing

Privacy

Only the values in your consent list leave this boat, and the deny list in the plugin settings always wins over anything the cloud asks for. When it connects, the connector also sends the boat's name and MMSI and a list of what this server has (path names, no values). What leaves the boat
